# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: we pledge to make participation a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. Be kind, assume good
faith, accept constructive criticism, and focus on what is best for the schools that use Maktab.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, publishing others' private information, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a
professional setting.

Report incidents to the maintainers at conduct@REPLACE-WITH-PROJECT-DOMAIN (placeholder). All
reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential. Maintainers may remove contributions or ban
contributors who violate this code, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
